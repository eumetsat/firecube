# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We are committed to making participation in Firecube welcoming, respectful, and
productive for everyone.

Contributors, maintainers, and users are expected to act in ways that support a
healthy technical community. That includes being kind, honest, constructive,
patient with different levels of experience, and willing to take responsibility
when something goes wrong.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that helps the community include:

- respecting the purpose of the project and the spaces where project work happens;
- using welcoming and inclusive language;
- respecting different viewpoints, backgrounds, and experiences;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- focusing criticism on ideas, code, and behavior rather than people;
- acknowledging mistakes and working to repair harm when appropriate;
- prioritizing the health of the community over personal disagreements.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- harassment, intimidation, threats, or sustained disruption;
- insulting, demeaning, or personal attacks;
- unwelcome sexual attention, imagery, or language;
- discriminatory language or behavior;
- publishing private information without explicit permission;
- abusing project processes, review channels, or maintainer time;
- other conduct that maintainers reasonably judge inappropriate in a
  professional project community.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull
requests, code review, documentation, chat, meetings, and project
events. It also applies when someone is officially representing the project in
public spaces.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior to the project maintainers. For private reports,
use the maintainer contact information in `AUTHORS.md`, or the private reporting
channel provided by the hosting platform if one is available.

Reports should include enough context for maintainers to understand what
happened, where it happened, and who was involved. Maintainers will handle
reports as promptly and fairly as possible and will respect the privacy and
security of reporters.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may take any action they judge necessary and proportionate to
protect the community. Possible actions include:

- clarifying expectations privately;
- asking for an apology or corrective action;
- warning the person responsible;
- moderating, editing, or removing comments, commits, issues, or other
  contributions;
- temporarily limiting participation;
- permanently banning a person from project spaces.

Maintainers who are involved in an incident should recuse themselves from
enforcement decisions when practical.
